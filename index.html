About the App

Stop keeping it all in your head

Anxiety Reducer is a repeating reminders and TODO app designed to help you stay on top of your life without the mental load. Capture tasks, set recurring reminders, and let the app remember things for you — so you don't have to.

Privacy

Your privacy matters

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Here's what you should know:

  • We do not collect any personally identifiable information.
  • Your data is stored exclusively in your personal iCloud account, so it stays private and syncs across your own devices.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We never sell your data to any third party.

If you have privacy-related questions, please reach out via the contact form below.
